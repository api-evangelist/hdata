Legislative Data Catalogs and Regulatory Intelligence in One Platform Thousands of bills move through Congress and the 50 state legislatures every year. For energy professionals, only a fraction of them matter: the ones that could reshape rates, siting, reliability standards, or market structure. Finding that fraction has meant checking dozens of separate legislative websites, tracking each one by hand and, too often, catching a bill only after it has already advanced.
