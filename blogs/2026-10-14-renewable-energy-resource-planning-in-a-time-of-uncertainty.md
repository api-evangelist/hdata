Designing Resource Plans Around Uncertainty Rather Than Against It Renewable energy has outgrown its identity as a niche addition to the grid. The question is no longer whether renewables will play a central role in meeting demand, but it’s how to plan for that future responsibly when the conditions shaping it refuse to hold still.
