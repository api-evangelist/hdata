How Southern Company Gas Uses Regulatory AI to Prepare for the Witness Stand Preparing to testify in a rate case is among the most demanding tasks in utility regulatory work. A single proceeding can run more than a year, with filings arriving in successive rounds. By the time a witness takes the stand, they may need to reconstruct their thinking on questions they last addressed five years earlier.
