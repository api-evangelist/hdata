Technology and AI in Regulated Energy Regulatory teams in energy face two pressures at once: the volume of filings keeps growing, and the deadlines don't move. Researching dockets, analyzing testimony, and turning it into a position still takes days the calendar doesn't always allow. AI promises relief, so the question comes up fast: should we build our own tool or buy one?
